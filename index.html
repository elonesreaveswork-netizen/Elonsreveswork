What Is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) describes computer systems designed to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, understanding language, and solving problems.

What About SI?

Artificial Intelligence

AI already helps people create, learn, communicate, research, and solve real-world problems.

Super Intelligence

Super Intelligence is a proposed concept for intelligence that could exceed human abilities across many important fields. It remains a subject of research and debate.

The Big Question

As these systems advance, how should we describe them, understand their limits, and make sure they benefit humanity?

Should We Move From AI to SI?

Some people believe the term “Super Intelligence” could better describe a possible future of increasingly capable intelligent systems. Others believe AI remains the clearest and most accurate term today.
